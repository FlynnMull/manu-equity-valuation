# Manchester United plc (NYSE: MANU) — Equity Valuation

Self-directed DCF valuation of Manchester United plc.

## Summary

- **Rating:** Underweight / Hold
- **12-Month Target Price:** $14.31 (vs. $20.90 current) — implies -31.5% downside
- **Methodology:** Unlevered DCF with dual terminal value approaches (Gordon 
  Growth and 12.5x exit EBITDA multiple), CAPM/Hamada-derived WACC (7.70%), 
  2-way sensitivity analysis, trading comps, and precedent transaction 
  cross-check (INEOS/Ratcliffe stake).

## Key Findings

The DCF fair value sits well below MANU's current trading price. This gap 
reflects a "trophy asset" premium in public markets — the stock behaves more 
as a proxy for a future control-premium buyout than a traditional 
cash-flow-generating equity, anchored by the $33/share INEOS precedent for 
their 28.9% stake.

Free cash flow generation remains structurally constrained by player 
amortisation (£190m run-rate) and net transfer capex (£140m p.a.), with 
UEFA Champions League qualification identified as the single most critical 
near-term catalyst for the forecast.
